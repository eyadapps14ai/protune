# Protune Auto Services identity

Selected concept: **01 / Precision monogram**. An angular P inside a six-sided badge combines precision with short motion lines. The manually drawn, slanted PROTUNE wordmark is balanced with a small, widely spaced AUTO SERVICES descriptor. All letters are vector paths, so font installation and font licensing are not required for these logo files.

## Master files

- `protune-horizontal-gold.svg` / `.png`: light wordmark and gold symbol for dark backgrounds.
- `protune-horizontal-black.svg` / `.png`: single black artwork for light backgrounds and monochrome printing.
- `protune-icon-gold.svg` / `.png`: transparent compact badge for social profiles and small placements.
- `protune-icon-black.svg` / `.png`: black compact badge.
- `favicon.svg` / `.png`: compact badge on an intentional rounded dark background.
- `concept-1-*`, `concept-2-*`, `concept-3-*`: all three concepts in gold/light and black; SVG and transparent PNG.

Horizontal PNGs: 1800 × 456 pixels. Compact PNGs: 408 × 432 pixels. The SVG masters scale without a fixed resolution. All PNGs other than the favicon retain transparent backgrounds.

## Colors

Gold `#C8A96B`, light `#F5F1E9`, black `#111111`, dark tile `#101110`.

Use solid gold, not gradient effects, for the logo itself. A printer can interpret the gold as metallic foil or a matched spot ink after a physical proof. Screens cannot guarantee a metallic print finish.

## Application

Keep at least the width of the wordmark's P stem as clear space around the artwork. Use the horizontal mark at 240 CSS pixels wide or larger where the descriptor must read clearly. At smaller sizes, use the compact icon; practical digital icon minimum is 24 pixels. For embroidery and small invoices, use the single-color black artwork or convert it to a single suitable thread/ink color. Confirm minimum stroke widths with the production supplier. Do not distort, rotate, outline, add shadows, or place the light/gold version on a light background.

These are original geometry and lettering created for this project. No stock clip art, external font, or brand insignia is embedded. Commercial trademark clearance is a separate business decision.
